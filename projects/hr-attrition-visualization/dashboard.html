What this sample shows

  • 237 of 1,470 records are marked as left (16.1%).
  • Sales has the highest department rate in this sample at 20.6% (446 records).
  • The overtime group shows 30.5% attrition versus 10.4% without overtime; this is an association in a fictional dataset, not evidence of cause.
  • Sales Representative has the highest job-role rate at 39.8% (83 records), so compare group sizes alongside rates.
  • The low job-satisfaction group is at 22.8%; ratings describe the sample and do not establish why employees left.
